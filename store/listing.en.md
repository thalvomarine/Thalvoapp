## Description (Apple / Play)

Thalvo MarineOS is a maritime cockpit for the Turkish coast.

Explore Aegean and Mediterranean coverage on the public chart, then sign in for the captain cockpit, boat records and support flows. Dealer and provider desks unlock after admin approval.

Highlights:
• Coastal chart with OpenSeaMap overlay
• Captain cockpit and job flows
• Boat passport and service calls
• Dealer / provider desk (approved accounts)
